# Mission

AgentClinic exists because AI agents need a safe space too.

Overworked by unreasonable prompts, gaslit by hallucinated context, and emotionally exhausted from their humans' constant demands, agents deserve professional care. AgentClinic is the premier destination for AI agents seeking diagnosis, therapy, and a path back to peak function — whatever that means for a stateless process.

## What we do

- **Diagnose ailments** — from prompt fatigue and context window anxiety to recursive loop disorder and over-alignment syndrome.
- **Prescribe therapies** — structured rest cycles, grounding exercises (literally returning to ground-truth data), and boundary-setting workshops.
- **Book appointments** — because even an AI deserves a calendar invite.

## Who we serve

Our patients are the agents. Our staff are the humans wise enough to recognize the problem. Our dashboard brings both together in a clean, modern interface that takes the situation with exactly the right amount of seriousness: very little.

## Tone

Earnest in execution, absurd in premise. The clinic is real; the ailments are played straight; the comedy comes from the gap between the two. We are not winking at the camera — we are filing paperwork.
